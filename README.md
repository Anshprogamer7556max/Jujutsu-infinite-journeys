# 🥋 My Jujutsu Infinite Journey

This repository is the story of my entire Jujutsu Infinite adventure.

I created it because one of my original ChatGPT conversations, which contained months of my journey, was accidentally lost. I don't want my story to disappear again.

So from this point onward, this repository is my permanent journal.

---

# Chapter 1 — The Beginning

My Jujutsu Infinite journey began on my account **"shivanshnoob7"**.

Like every new player, I started with the free spins the game provides. The game gave me **20 spins**, and by joining the official group I received **50 more**, giving me a total of **70 spins**.

I had no idea how lucky I was about to be.

Within my first **30 spins**, I rolled **Star Rage**, a **Special Grade Innate**. At the time, I didn't realize how valuable it was, but the community considered it one of the best all-around innates in the game. I only began to understand its strength when I noticed that quests felt much easier than I expected.

From there, I kept grinding. Day after day, I progressed through the game, becoming stronger and reaching **Level 271** on my original account.

During my journey, however, I occasionally experienced strange movement issues. Sometimes my character would unexpectedly dash into walls, and other times I would be ragdolled because of network problems. I didn't think much of it at the time.

Then one day, after returning home from school, everything changed.

I logged in expecting another normal session, only to find that my account had been **banned for "Exploiting."**

I was shocked.

I had never expected to see that message, and I couldn't understand why it had happened.

I refused to give up immediately. I contacted the Jujutsu Infinite support team, searched for help through Discord, and tried every official method I could find in hopes of getting my account back.

Despite all of my efforts, I received no response that resolved the situation.

At that moment, months of progress, my original account, and the journey I had built all seemed to disappear in an instant.

It was one of the lowest points of my Jujutsu Infinite journey—but it was not the end.

# Chapter 2 — A New Beginning

After losing my original account, I spent some time thinking about whether I should continue playing Jujutsu Infinite at all.

The ban wasn't my fault, but it had taken away everything I had worked for. Starting over from nothing felt unfair.

Even so, I realized something important.

I wasn't attached to my old account—I was attached to **Jujutsu Infinite itself**. I still enjoyed the game, and I wasn't ready to leave it behind because of something I couldn't control.

So I made a decision.

Instead of giving up, I would start again.

I used my younger brother's Roblox account, **"anshpro3737,"** and began a completely new journey from scratch.

There were no high levels waiting for me, no rare items, and no progress carried over from before. Everything I achieved from this point onward would have to be earned again.

Looking back now, this wasn't just another restart.

**This was the true beginning of my Jujutsu Infinite journey.**

Everything that follows in this archive happened on this account. Every achievement, every challenge, every rare drop, every trade, every victory, and every lesson belongs to the story that started here.

This time, I wasn't just playing to reach the endgame.

I was writing a new story.
